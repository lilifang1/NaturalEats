High Protein Mac and Cheese


This easy 10-minute recipe makes two protein-packed wraps that are perfect for lunch or dinner. Made with a creamy cottage cheese and tuna mix, fresh tomato, red onion, sliced avocado, and melty Monterey Jack, all wrapped in a high-protein tortilla. Quick to make and seriously satisfying. Don't worry it's not super spicy, the spice level is about a 1/10!



Nutrition Facts


  • ~ 410 calories

  • ~ 39g protein

  • ~ 14g fat

  • ~ 35g net carbs


Ingredients




  • 1/2 box Barilla Protein+ Penne (4oz dry)

  • 1/2 tbsp dijon

  • 1 tsp salt, garlic powder, onion powder

  • 2 cups low sodium bone broth

  • 1/2 cup low fat cottage cheese

  • 1/2 cup low-calorie cheddar cheese

  • 2 tbsp nutritional yeast

Steps to Make

  • In a blender, blend your cottage cheese, dijon, and seasonings until smooth with no clumps

  • Add to a pot on low heat and mix in your cheddar cheese



  • Add 2 cups bone broth and 2 cups water to a pot

  • Boil your noodles in them for 8 minutes or until al dente

  • Drain noodles and combine with cheese sauce

  • Enjoy!!
